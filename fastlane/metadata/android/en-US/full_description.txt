Stuck at the same weight for weeks? Your log already knows why.

Dale is a personal trainer and nutritionist in your pocket. No ads, no account, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

PLAN
• Answer a few questions (goal, build, experience, days a week) and get a full training plan.
• Five trainer profiles: Hardgainer, Cutter, Powerlifter, Recomp and Engine.
• Train in the gym, at home with dumbbells, or with calisthenics. Switch any time.
• Every exercise reads "4 sets of 8–12 reps", and you can adjust sets and reps. Dale remembers.

EXERCISES
• 30 exercises with 3D demos that turn from side view to 45° to front view.
• Add your own: describe it or record a short video. Dale compares it with the exercises it knows, places it in the right muscle group and turns your video into a stick-figure demo.

COACH
• Log your weights and Dale suggests next session's load.
• Rate each session. Based on your last two weeks, the coach suggests a lighter week, fewer days, a level-up or a new block.

NUTRITION
• Daily calories and macros for training and rest days.
• Four meals a day with full recipes, scaled to your target.
• A 7-day grocery list.

TRACKING
• Check-ins, streaks and a 16-week attendance calendar.
• A reminder on training days if you haven't checked in. It can check you in automatically at your saved gym.
